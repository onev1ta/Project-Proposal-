About Features Why Worth It?

Is this restaurant worth visiting?

Worth It? is a visual, social way to discover restaurants, rate your visits, and recommend your favorites to others.

About the Project

People hear about restaurants on TikTok, Instagram, Yelp, and Google, but still struggle to find recommendations they can trust. Worth It? combines the detailed ratings of review sites with the visual, fun feel of social media, so choosing where to eat feels easy instead of overwhelming.

Key Features

Restaurant Search

Find places by name, location, or type of cuisine.

Ratings & Reviews

Rate a restaurant and share your experience after your visit.

Restaurant Profiles

See photos, ratings, reviews, location, and cuisine in one place.

Why Worth It?

Yelp offers depth but can feel cluttered. TikTok makes discovery fun but isn't consistent. Worth It? aims to blend both: clean restaurant cards, clear ratings, and recommendations from real diners.
